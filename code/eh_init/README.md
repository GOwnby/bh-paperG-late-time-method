# Event-horizon-regular data (Paper G, Section 3 and following)

Run every script from this directory after `sh ../build.sh`. The commands that generated the JSON data sets are in `MANIFEST.md`; the table of results, scripts and run times is in the top-level `../../README.md`.

Section numbers in the round headings below (Sec. 6.x) refer to the 39-page Paper E from which Paper G was split (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23455587); in Paper G they are Sections 3-5. e15_extend.py, e8_semiclassical.py, raylib_sc.py and their results are Paper E material kept for completeness and are not used by Paper G.

Purpose: remove the gauge caveat of the double-null runs (sigma static on the initial rays => sigma_v -> -kappa_-(static) by construction).
Data are regular on H+ = {U=0}, U = exp(kappa_+ u), with the exterior advanced time v; the tail phi(0,v)=B(1+v)^-k is prescribed on H+.

Build the kernels (the .so files are not shipped): `sh ../build.sh` (stored-array driver `libdouble_null.so` from `double_null.c`, streaming drivers `libstream.so`, `libstream2.so`). stream.c is a two-row streaming copy of double_null.c; its output is bitwise identical on a test run (max difference 0.0) and its memory is O(n_v).

- Logs: results_eh_init.txt (static validation, tail-only runs, mucheck), results_tailonly_collapse.txt (B=0.05 and k=1/2 tail-only; first-NaN column for B=0.03,0.04,0.05),

Status of the claims (see the paper for the full statement)
1. Static (B=0): second order in sigma, sigma_v to v=100. r_U, r_v in the interior are noise-level and not used.
2. Tail only, k=3/2: sigma_v on U=0.3 -> -kappa_-(mu_f) to 0.03/0.05/0.08% (B=0.03/0.04/0.05), predicted shifts from static 1.4/2.5/4.0%. k=1/2: 0.27% off and not in the asymptotic regime.
3. Raw M is noise-dominated on every single grid (sign and size change with h; the truncation-error mass is (1.5-1.7)e-3 (h_s^2+h_v^2)); the five-grid Romberg extrapolation is positive, its two highest levels agree to 1.1e-4, and it is used (tail-only included).
4. Five-grid runs to v=108 (kappa_- v ~ 85): the differential Ori relation d ln M/dv = lambda - lambda'/lambda, lambda=|sigma_v|+2 d ln|phi_v|/dv, holds to <=2e-4 in the rate for windows [72,76]...[104,108] (tail-only k=1/2, 3/2; seeded k=1/2, 3/2, 5/2; rays U=0.6, 1.0, 1.2), to 2e-3 at [60,64] for seeded runs. Seed scan (k=3/2): (A,U0,w)=(0.01,1,0.3) and (0.02,0.7,0.2) agree from v=72; (0.02,1,0.5) fails near the node of phi_v at v~92 (as the derivation predicts); (0.03,0.6,0.3) is non-perturbative (r<0 on U=1 by v~60).
   The ingoing flux on the rays is 7-110 times the transported tail (3e3 for seeded k=5/2; 1.4 and 9.5 times T(0) f at v=100 for k=1/2, 3/2), nearly ray independent, and its local exponent is not a power law in the window (k=1/2: 2.0-2.2; k=3/2: 3.0 -> 4.6); the exponent 2k+2 in M is tested only by the exact ray integration (below). |sigma_v| -> kappa_-(mu_f): k=3/2 0.06% above at v~106 (U=1); k=1/2 0.66% above and falling (U=0.3: stationary excess 2e-3).
   The earlier 3-grid seeded results (seed5.py, diffrate5.py, v<=76) are superseded; their files are kept for the record.
5. r<0 appears at U > U_c(B) (about 3.6, 3.3, 2.9 for B=0.03, 0.04, 0.05; first-NaN v depends on h); spacelike character and a threshold in B are not established.
Withdrawn: an earlier seeded variant kept r static on v=0 and violated the u-constraint by 25-30% of the source term; its scripts and numbers were removed.

Ray round (Paper E Secs. 6.8-6.9; field-equation check)

Constraint-projection round (Paper E Sec. 6.9, Table tab:proj, Fig. fig_e13; Roadmap E13, E3b, E11, E14)
- stream2.c / libstream2.so (build: gcc -O3 -fPIC -shared stream2.c -o libstream2.so, no fast-math): stream.c plus the cumulative trapezoid I(s)=int_0^s 4 pi r phi_s^2 e^{-sigma} ds along each row (extra output outI). gen3.py `B k A U0 w v1 h1 ...` stores M, I and the eight fields at ray positions placed on grid points of the coarsest grid (no interpolation): gen3_B{B}_k{k}_v{v1}.json. Runs: B=0.03 with k=0.5, 1.5, 2, 3 and B=0.003 with k=0.5, 1.5, v1=300, five grids h=0.05...0.003125 (about 10 min for the finest each); gen3_B0.03_k1.5_v110.json is the shorter run.
- e13_project.py: the constraint-projected mass. y_c = 1/2 + I (exact, positive integrand; follows from d_u(r_u/W) = -4 pi r phi_u^2/W), rho and r from the two v-Raychaudhuri equations started at v0=60 (starting anywhere in 48..84 changes ln M_cc by <=1e-10), M_cc = (r/2)(1+4 y_c rho). run() returns the ray-ODE solution calibrated at v0 (solA) and the constraint solution (solC).
- e13_all.py `gen3_...json [ray]` -> results_e13_B*_k*.txt (rays n=2: U=0.9993, n=0: U=0.3003): ln M_cc - ln M_ODE, grid-subset spreads, next-level I difference, lower bound ln(16 M^2/r^6) on the Kretschmann scalar. e13_errconst.py -> results_e13_errconst.txt (raw error constants C_rho(v), C_y per grid, pure h^2). e13_table.py prints (and writes to frag11/rows.tex) the LaTeX rows of Table 5; its output is results_e13_table.txt; e13_fig.py -> fig_e13.png in the current directory (copy to the repository root to rebuild the PDF).
- E11 (matching factor of the kernel flux): transfer_mu.py / transfer_vec_mu.py (MU_F=<mu_f> environment variable: transfer function of the hole of the accumulated mass), kernel_mu.py -> results_kernel_mu.txt, kernel_lib_mu.py (KERNEL_MU=<mu_f>; caches K_mu_<value>.npy), e11_ratio.py -> results_e11_ratio.txt (PDE/kernel flux ratio vs v). ray_extend.py now takes KERNEL_MU to use the final-hole kernel; results_ray_extend_ext_def_* (static-hole kernel) and results_ray_extend_ext_mu_* (final-hole kernel) cover B=0.03 k=0.5,1.5,2,3 and B=0.003 k=0.5,1.5.
- Claims: M_cc and the ray ODE agree to 1.4e-8 ... 2.3e-6 in ln M for 84<=v<=280 (M up to e^179); the raw evolved PDE mass cannot be made convergent at late v (second-order error of rho = -r_v grows with v). Not claimed: that this tests the equations (M_cc uses the same extrapolated sigma_v, phi_v); k=3 M_cc is good only to ~1e-6; beyond v~250 the flux is a linear-response model; matching-factor remainder 0.6-0.7% (k>=3/2), 1.9% (k=1/2) unexplained.
